The Fear & Greed Index is a daily score from 0 to 100, published by CNN. It measures how nervous or confident US stock investors are, using seven signals, such as how fast prices are moving and how much investors are paying to protect themselves against losses.

Below 25 is extreme fear. Above 75 is extreme greed. In the past, extreme fear has often shown up near market bottoms, and extreme greed near market tops. It doesn't predict what happens next.

The S&P 500 tracks the 500 largest US companies, so it's a common stand-in for the US stock market as a whole.

“Be fearful when others are greedy, and greedy when others are fearful.”Warren Buffett
